The loop

Record. Convert. Run.

Three steps, no code. The same loop a non-programmer admin runs, and the same one a modder extends.

01 / RECORD

You drive the route

Get in any vehicle, hit one key, and drive it like you always would — stops, reversals, everything. Reverse and direction changes are read from the gear.

NUMPAD 5
02 / CONVERT

The wizard reads the car

It builds the route from your take, reading the vehicle's declared physics — torque curve, gearbox, steering. No per-vehicle tuning, no training.

config-as-manual
03 / RUN

An NPC drives it

Load it from the live panel and Boris runs it instantly — no server restart. Watch every vehicle, pause it, reposition it.

Reproductor
Generalization

One controller. Every vehicle.

Hand a line to a sedan, a sports car, a 4x4, or a truck — the controller reads each car's own physics (engine, gearbox, steering) and drives it. Each takes the line by its own limits: the small car nails it, the truck slower but gets there. Vanilla or modded — anything built on CarScript.

Telemetry

Parks to the centimeter.

The final stop self-configures per vehicle and surface — no per-route constants. It reads the car's brake torque and the real grip under the wheels.

These are out-of-sample takes, one config, forward and reverse. Even the historically-hard case — stopping right after a curve — is resolved.

Under the hood

Real control, not a scripted teleport.

The driver applies genuine steering, throttle, and brake on the simulated vehicle — frame by frame.

Config-as-manual

Reads, doesn't learn

Instead of training on data, it reads the physics the vehicle already declares. No machine learning — deterministic and inspectable.

Pure-pursuit

Follows the line

Aims at a point ahead on your path, sets speed from the curvature coming up, derives gear from an inverse model of the engine.

Auto-detect

Reverse & K-turns

You mark nothing. The converter reads the gear: direction changes and reversals are detected on their own.

Live panel

Run routes hot

Load and run routes with no restart. See every vehicle driving, pause it, reposition it, stop it — from one dashboard.

Route editor

Draw on the map

A visual editor to draw, join, and fine-tune routes over the terrain — bundled in the package.

Graph routing

Compose the map

Join many takes into a graph and route A→B pairs no one ever drove end-to-end. Plus a scenario DSL and Quests integration.

Get started

Add it, drive, done.

Add @BZ_AutoDrive to your active mods, bind the three keys, and record your first route. Full walkthrough in the manual.
